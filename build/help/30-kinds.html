Kinds of figure

trace-digitiser has a reader for each kind of figure. Choose by what the values are drawn as, not by what the figure is about.

KindForPython · command
Lineslines and curves: time courses, spectra, survival curves, traces of recordingstd.digitise · trace-digitiser FIG
Pointsmarkers of any shape, with or without error bars: scatter plots, dot plots, forest plots, lines with markerstd.digitise_points · trace-digitiser points FIG
Barsbar charts, up or across, up from or down from the baseline, with error barstd.digitise_bars · trace-digitiser bars FIG
Rowsa stack of rows: actograms, double-plotted records, birthograms, raster plotstd.digitise_rows · trace-digitiser rows FIG
Heat mapcells coloured on a colour bar (or a known colour map)td.digitise_heatmap · trace-digitiser heatmap FIG
Polarpoints on polar axes: phases, circular data, rose plots' pointstd.digitise_polar · trace-digitiser polar FIG

A figure with lines and markers (a line through its points) can be read either way: as points, if what matters is the points; as lines, for the values between them. Box plots and pie charts have no reader yet: their few values are quickest read with the mouse – the status bar gives the value under it once two ticks are marked.
