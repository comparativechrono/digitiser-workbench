FunctionGives
td.digitise(image, …)lines: result.trace_data, .panels, .warnings, .provenance, .resample(interval), .overlay(), .plot()
td.digitise_points, _bars, _rows, _heatmap, _polara FigureResult: .data, .panels, .warnings, .provenance, .csv_path, .overlay()
td.describe_figure(image)what is found: plot areas, tick marks, labels read and the scales they give, colours, a colour bar, polar axes
td.pixels_to_values(points, x_ref=…, y_ref=…)pixel positions in the axes' units
td.find_polar_axes(rgb)a polar plot's centre, radius and rings

Options every reader but polar takes

plot_area, exclude (pixel boxes: left, top, right, bottom), y_ref, x_ref (lists of (pixel, value), or a dict of them by panel number), y_min/y_max, x_min/x_max, y_scale/x_scale ("auto", "linear", "log"), y_axis="right", ocr=False, straighten ("auto", False or degrees), output_dir, write_outputs=False, save_overlay=True. Then each reader's own: help(td.digitise_rows) lists them.

The full documentation is trace-digitiser's README, on GitHub.
